Credit. This site's daily reading plan was inspired by A Man After God's Own Heart: Devoting Your Life to What Really Matters by Jim George and Elizabeth George (2002), which includes a one-year daily Bible reading plan.

Reading time. The book describes this plan as averaging about 12 minutes a day. Measuring the real word count of every day's reading (via the same passage source this site uses) at a typical 200-words-per-minute silent-reading pace, the actual daily average comes out to roughly 10 minutes — close to the book's figure, and consistent with it if read at a slightly more deliberate, reflective pace (around 170 wpm) rather than brisk silent reading. Individual days vary widely, from under a minute to nearly 24 minutes, depending on the chapters assigned.
